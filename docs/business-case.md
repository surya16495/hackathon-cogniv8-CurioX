## Use case
See the Use case and Impact sections of the [README](../README.md): busy professionals keeping up with their field, students preparing for exams and interviews, people who learn by talking, and newcomers who do not yet know what to ask. Today they bounce off long articles, generic feeds and chatbots that answer in walls of text. CurioX gives them a three-minute daily issue of cards on exactly their topics, answers as cards with a quiz and a teach-back, and a spoken conversation that remembers what they cared about.
